*** How Org-mode & =org-roam= Fix That Obsidian Flow
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: how-org-mode-org-roam-fix-that-obsidian-flow
:END:
In Emacs with Evil-mode (or Kakoune/Helix keys mapped):

1. *Creating & Linking Stubs Instantly:* Press =Space + m + l= (or
   =Org-capture= / =org-roam-node-insert=). You type the name of the
   note---if it doesn't exist, it creates the file in the background,
   drops a backlink right under your cursor, and lets you keep typing
   without ever reaching for a mouse or losing your focus.
2. *Atomic TODO Backlinking:* Instead of manually pasting bullet lists
   and typing =[[DATE-TODO]]=, Org-mode has native *agenda scheduling
   and state toggling*. Tapping =t= toggles any headline from =TODO=
   \(\rightarrow\) =DOING= \(\rightarrow\) =DONE=, automatically
   stamping dates and linking them back to your global agenda file.
3. *Zero Context Switching:* You stay right inside your terminal frame
   (or native GUI window) next to your code, using your exact home-row
   navigation.
